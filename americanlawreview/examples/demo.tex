\section{Citation State}

The first note gives a full case citation.\lrfootnote{\See \casecite[347]{brown}.}
The next note repeats the same authority.\lrfootnote{\casecite[349]{brown}.}
A mixed note keeps separate authorities in the same footnote.\lrfootnote{\See
\statcite{alistat}; \artcite[45]{coase}; \bookcite[12]{hart}.}
The article later becomes a supra note.\lrfootnote{\artcite[46]{coase}.}
The case later uses a legal short form, not a supra note.\lrfootnote{\casecite[350]{brown}.}
A shorthand source may be introduced.\lrfootnote{\bluecite[23]{restatement}.}
The shorthand repeats compactly.\lrfootnote{\bluecite[24]{restatement}.}
