Citation State

The first note gives a full case citation.1 The next note repeats the same authority.2 A
mixed note keeps separate authorities in the same footnote.3 The article later becomes a
supra note.4 The case later uses a legal short form, not a supra note.5 A shorthand source
may be introduced.6 The shorthand repeats compactly.7

∗

The author note is printed as a title footnote and reset before body notes.
